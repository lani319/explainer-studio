# Review checklist

Run `stills` with at least one mark per section for every language, then check:

**Content**
- [ ] Every statement matches the source; quotes are exact; translations are marked unofficial.
- [ ] Sources appear on the closing scene.
- [ ] Nothing confidential or personal slipped in from the material.

**Picture**
- [ ] No text runs outside its box or into another element; long titles shrank instead of overflowing.
- [ ] Content stays above the subtitle (y ≈ 840).
- [ ] CJK text shows real glyphs, not boxes; Chinese uses Chinese forms, Japanese uses Japanese forms.
- [ ] The highlighted item is the one being spoken about.

**Sound and subtitles**
- [ ] No `warn` lines from `build` (or each one is understood).
- [ ] Names, numbers and abbreviations are pronounced correctly (spot-check in the browser player).
- [ ] The `.srt` opens in a player and matches the speech.
